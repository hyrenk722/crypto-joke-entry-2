Entry for the make.fun competition "Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest": one joke about
crypto and trading life, a single line under 200 characters. `main.py` writes that joke
to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the
gate first: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one
line — then reading the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> I told my wife crypto is a long-term investment, which is technically true — I've been losing money on it for years.

Why this joke: it is true to trading life twice over — "long-term investment" is the line every
crypto holder tells their family, and years of losses are what most of them actually have —
and the turn pivots twice, first on "technically true" and then on the payoff that the
long term has only meant losing money for years. The domestic setup makes it a line a
trader would actually repeat to a friend, the punchline lands on the last word, and the
whole line reads cleanly aloud in one breath.
